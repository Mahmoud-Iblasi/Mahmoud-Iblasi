### 👨‍💻 &nbsp;About me

- 🔭 Software Engineer at **Palmira ITC**, building **MetrixPlus**, a multi-tenant KPI and performance-management SaaS platform of about 13 Spring Boot microservices.
- ⚙️ Core developer of its **KPI calculation engine** (Java 17, Spring Boot 3). I own the calculation, workflow and metadata services.
- 🧩 I enjoy the hard parts of backend work: multi-tenancy, background jobs that stay safe across servers, workflow automation, and evolving shared contracts without breaking the services that use them.
- 🌱 Full-stack (Angular + Spring) from 2021 to 2024, backend-focused since.
- 🔒 Most of my work lives in private company repositories, so this profile shows only a small part of it.
- 📫 Reach me at **iblasi.mahmoud@gmail.com** · Jordan · open to remote roles.
